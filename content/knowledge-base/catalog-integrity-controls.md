Catalog integrity controls are the records and routines that keep your product listings correct: an approved source of truth, a log of every change, regular checks of the live pages, and saved evidence you can show a marketplace when something is wrong.

They matter because on Amazon several parties can contribute data to the same detail page, so listings can change without your team touching them, and you need proof to get them corrected quickly.

## Why do listings change without your input?

An Amazon detail page is shared. Every seller of the same product sells on one page, and Amazon builds that page from multiple data sources. Brand Registry gives the brand owner more influence over what is displayed, but it does not make the page untouchable. Common causes of unwanted changes:

- Another seller submits different data (title, images, attributes)
- Amazon's systems adjust attributes or the category
- A bulk upload or template error on your own side
- Variation families merged or split
- Deliberate abuse, such as swapped images or a hijacked listing

## What controls should you have in place?

| Control | What it is | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Source of truth | The approved title, bullets, attributes and image files for every SKU, kept outside Seller Central | Lets you spot drift and restore content fast |
| Change log | Each edit with date, owner, reason, before/after and rollback step | Separates your changes from outside ones; explains performance shifts |
| Access control | Named user accounts with the least permissions needed in Seller Central and Brand Registry | Knowing who can change what; no shared logins |
| Live-page audit | Regular comparison of live pages with the source of truth | Finds unwanted changes before they cost sales |
| Evidence archive | Dated screenshots, files and documents proving what is correct | Supports correction requests and violation reports |
| Brand Registry enrollment | Registered trademark linked to the brand in Amazon Brand Registry | Stronger control over detail pages and access to reporting tools |

## What evidence should you collect?

Marketplaces act faster on clear evidence. Keep these on file for every product:

- **Ownership proof:** trademark registration numbers, Brand Registry details
- **Product proof:** GTIN/UPC records registered to the brand, spec sheets, packaging artwork, label files
- **Approved content:** the image files and copy as approved, with dates
- **Live-page snapshots:** dated screenshots of each detail page after each approved change, with the URL visible
- **Change records:** your change log, including upload confirmations
- **Compliance documents:** safety data sheets, certificates and test reports where the category needs them

When an unwanted change happens, add:

- A screenshot of the incorrect page, with date and URL
- The matching "correct" version from your source of truth
- Any seller ID linked to the change, if visible

## How do you correct an unwanted change?

1. **Confirm it is not yours.** Check your change log and recent uploads first.
2. **Capture the evidence.** Screenshot the live page and save the correct version alongside it.
3. **Resubmit the correct data.** Update the listing through your Brand Registry-linked account so your contribution carries the brand owner's weight.
4. **Open a case if it doesn't stick.** Use Seller Central or Brand Registry support, attach your evidence, and state exactly which fields are wrong and what they should be.
5. **Report abuse separately.** If another seller is deliberately misusing your brand content or trademark, use the Report a Violation tool in Brand Registry.
6. **Recheck and log.** Confirm the fix on the live page and record the case number and outcome.

## Weekly integrity checklist

- Top ASINs compared with the source of truth (title, bullets, main image, key attributes)
- Variation families intact
- No new ASINs using your brand name that you did not create
- Brand Registry alerts and Brand Dashboard reviewed
- Change log up to date; every change has an owner
- Open correction cases followed up
- Seller Central user list reviewed; departed users removed

## FAQ

### Does Brand Registry stop other sellers from changing my listing?

It gives the brand owner more say over what is displayed, but it does not lock the page. Monitoring is still needed.

### How long should I keep evidence?

As long as the product is sold, plus a reasonable period after. Disputes can reference old versions of a page.

### What is a single source of truth?

One approved record of every listing's content kept outside the marketplace, such as a controlled spreadsheet or product information system.

### Is this the same as catalog hygiene?

They overlap. Hygiene keeps data correct and complete; integrity controls prove what is correct and defend it when it changes.
