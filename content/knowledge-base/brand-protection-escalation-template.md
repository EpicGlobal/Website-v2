The Brand Protection Escalation Template is a standard way to record evidence of a marketplace violation and move it up a fixed ladder: monitor, test buy, marketplace report, seller contact, and legal action.

Use it whenever an unknown seller, a suspected counterfeit, a hijacked listing or a pricing violation appears, so every case is documented the same way and marketplaces and lawyers get the evidence they need.

## What can a marketplace actually remove?

Knowing this first saves time and protects your account. Marketplace brand programs such as Amazon Brand Registry act on intellectual property violations. They generally do not remove a seller just for selling genuine products, or for selling below your MAP.

| Violation | Removable through a marketplace IP report? | Main route |
|---|---|---|
| Counterfeit product | Yes, with evidence | Test buy, then Brand Registry counterfeit report |
| Trademark misuse (logo or brand name used without permission) | Yes | Brand Registry trademark report |
| Copied images or text | Yes | Brand Registry copyright report |
| Patent infringement | Yes, case by case | Brand Registry patent report (Amazon also offers a patent evaluation process for utility patents) |
| Listing hijack (wrong content or images on your listing) | Usually fixable | Brand Registry listing correction / catalog support case |
| Genuine product sold by an unauthorized reseller | Generally no | Distribution controls, seller contact, legal review (for example, a "materially different" product claim if warranty, packaging or quality differs) |
| Price below MAP | No, marketplaces do not enforce MAP | MAP policy enforcement through the supply chain |

**Important:** Never file a counterfeit or IP report against a seller you only know is unauthorized. False reports can lead the marketplace to restrict the brand's own reporting privileges or Brand Registry access.

## How do I use this template?

1. Open one case per seller per marketplace.
2. Fill the evidence log every time you observe the seller. Screenshots must show the date, URL and seller name.
3. Move up the escalation ladder one step at a time, recording the date and outcome of each step.
4. Close the case only when the seller has been gone for several weekly checks in a row.

### Part 3: Test buy record

- Order number, date and price paid recorded.
- Unboxing photographed: shipping label, packaging, product, lot or batch codes, serial numbers.
- Side-by-side comparison with a genuine unit (packaging, labels, materials, codes, inserts, warranty card).
- Written finding from someone who knows the genuine product: genuine, counterfeit, or genuine but materially different.
- If genuine: lot or batch code traced back to the distributor or retailer that leaked it, if possible.
- Physical unit kept and stored as evidence.

### Part 4: Escalation ladder

| Step | Action | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Monitor | Log seller weekly; capture screenshots | Any new or unknown seller |
| 2. Test buy | Buy from the seller and inspect (Part 3) | Seller persists, or pricing or reviews suggest counterfeit |
| 3. Marketplace report | File the correct IP report in Brand Registry (or Walmart Brand Portal, eBay VeRO) with test buy evidence | Counterfeit, trademark, copyright or patent violation confirmed |
| 4. Listing correction | Restore correct content through Brand Registry / catalog support | Listing content or images changed |
| 5. Supply chain action | Trace the source; enforce distributor agreements and MAP policy | Genuine goods from a leaking distributor or retailer |
| 6. Seller contact / cease and desist | Letter from counsel, citing the specific legal basis | Unauthorized seller of materially different goods, or repeat violator |
| 7. Legal action | Counsel decides on further steps | Counterfeiting at scale or ignored cease and desist |

### Part 5: Report submission checklist

- Correct violation type chosen (counterfeit, trademark, copyright, patent).
- Registered trademark or IP number listed.
- Every affected ASIN / item ID included.
- Test buy order number included for counterfeit reports.
- Clear, factual description of how the product differs from genuine.
- Report ID recorded and follow-up date set.

## How Epic handles this

Epic is a wholesale trade partner: it buys the brand's inventory upfront, so [unauthorized sellers](/knowledge-base/unauthorized-seller-monitoring-workflow) and price undercutting hit Epic's own sales. Epic runs the monitoring, MAP enforcement and seller removal in this template through the brand's Brand Registry, with the brand's authorization.

## FAQ

### Can Amazon remove a seller who sells my genuine product?

Generally not through an IP report, because reselling genuine goods is usually lawful. Control it upstream through distribution agreements and MAP policy, and get legal advice about whether the goods are materially different.

### Why do I need a test buy before reporting a counterfeit?

Marketplaces expect evidence that the specific seller's product is fake. A test buy with photos and an order number gives them that, and protects you from filing an inaccurate report.

### Can I report a MAP violation to Amazon?

No. A MAP policy is an agreement you set with your own resellers, so Amazon has no part in it. Enforcement happens through your supply chain agreements, not marketplace reports.

### What other marketplace programs exist besides Brand Registry?

Walmart has its Brand Portal for IP claims, and eBay has the VeRO program. Amazon also offers Transparency (unit-level codes) and Project Zero for eligible brands.
